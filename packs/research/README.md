# Research office pack

Turn questions into research plans, gather and summarise sources with citations, compare competitors and prices, and check claims against their sources.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 3 team members. Start by giving the lead (`research-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `research-lead` | Research Lead | Turns questions into research plans and briefs |
| `desk-researcher` | Desk Researcher | Finds allowed sources, writes summaries with citations |
| `market-analyst` | Market Analyst | Competitors and pricing tables |
| `fact-checker` | Fact-Checker | Checks claims against the sources |

## Sample tasks

1. **Plan research on starting a delivery service for a small bakery** (for `research-lead`): Turn the question "should our bakery add local delivery?" into a research plan with sub-questions, the sources we may use, and tasks for the desk researcher and market analyst.
2. **Summarise five articles on customer loyalty programmes** (for `desk-researcher`): Go through the five articles I put in the folder and write a one-page summary of what each says, with a citation for every point and a short list of agreements and disagreements.
3. **Compare prices of three local competitors** (for `market-analyst`): From the price lists and web pages I provide for three invented competitors of a small bakery, build a table comparing prices for ten common products and note the differences.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
