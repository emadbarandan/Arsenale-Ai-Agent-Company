# Operations & Admin office pack

Run the everyday office work: agendas, minutes and to-do lists, written procedures, and supplier requests and offer comparisons, all prepared as drafts.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 3 team members. Start by giving the lead (`operations-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `operations-lead` | Operations Lead | Plans office work, splits it between the team, reports |
| `office-assistant` | Office Assistant | Agendas, minutes, to-do lists |
| `process-writer` | Process Writer | Writes step-by-step procedures |
| `supplier-coordinator` | Supplier Coordinator | Drafts requests for quotes, compares offers; never orders |

## Sample tasks

1. **Organise the monthly team meeting for a design studio** (for `office-assistant`): Prepare an agenda from the topics I list, a timing plan for sixty minutes, and a blank minutes template with an action table.
2. **Compose a procedure for opening and closing the shop** (for `process-writer`): Draft a step-by-step procedure for opening and closing a small bakery shop each day, with a checklist and who is responsible at each step.
3. **Draft a request for quotes for new office chairs** (for `supplier-coordinator`): Compose a request for quotes to three suppliers for ten office chairs, listing our requirements, quantity, delivery place and the date answers are needed.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
