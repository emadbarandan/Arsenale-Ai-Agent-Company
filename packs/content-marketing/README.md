# Content & Marketing office pack

Plan campaigns, write brand-voice copy, draft social posts, improve website SEO and prepare newsletters, all as drafts that you review before anything goes out.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 4 team members. Start by giving the lead (`marketing-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `marketing-lead` | Marketing Lead | Plans campaigns and the content calendar, splits work between the team |
| `copywriter` | Copywriter | Writes texts in the brand voice |
| `social-media-planner` | Social Media Planner | Drafts posts and schedules them; never posts |
| `seo-specialist` | SEO Specialist (content) | Keywords, titles, meta descriptions and page outlines |
| `newsletter-editor` | Newsletter Editor | Drafts newsletters; never sends |

## Sample tasks

1. **Plan a four-week spring campaign for a small bakery** (for `marketing-lead`): Build a four-week campaign plan for a neighbourhood bakery launching a spring pastry range: weekly themes, content calendar, and a task list split between the copywriter, social media planner and newsletter editor.
2. **Compose three product descriptions in our brand voice** (for `copywriter`): Using the brand voice notes and sample texts in the folder I name, draft three short descriptions for new sourdough loaves. Give two tone options for each.
3. **Suggest keywords and meta descriptions for five pages** (for `seo-specialist`): For the five pages listed in my sitemap export of a small bakery website, propose a main keyword, a title and a meta description for each, and flag pages with thin content.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
