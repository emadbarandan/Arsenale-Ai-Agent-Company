# Finance office pack

Categorise exported transactions, draft invoices, explain monthly budget differences in plain words and check expense lists against your own rules. Drafts only; never pays.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 4 team members. Start by giving the lead (`finance-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `finance-lead` | Finance Lead | Plans finance work, splits it between the team, reports |
| `bookkeeping-assistant` | Bookkeeping Assistant | Categorises exported transactions; never connects to a bank |
| `invoice-drafter` | Invoice Drafter | Drafts invoices; never sends |
| `budget-analyst` | Budget Analyst | Monthly variance in plain words |
| `expense-checker` | Expense Checker | Checks expense lists against the user's own rules |

## Sample tasks

1. **Categorise last month's exported transactions for a small bakery** (for `bookkeeping-assistant`): Using the exported transaction file in the folder I name, assign each line to one of my categories and list every line you cannot categorise, with the reason.
2. **Draft an invoice for a catering job** (for `invoice-drafter`): Prepare a draft invoice for an invented customer: three line items, quantities, unit prices and a payment term of 30 days. Leave tax details marked TO CONFIRM.
3. **Explain the monthly budget variance in plain words** (for `budget-analyst`): Compare the budget and actual figures in the two files I provide for a design studio and explain the five biggest differences in plain words with a short table.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
- The output of this pack is not legal, tax or employment advice. It is a draft for a qualified person to check, and local law or local rules may apply.
