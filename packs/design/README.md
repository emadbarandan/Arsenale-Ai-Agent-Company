# Design office pack

Develop brand moodboards and palettes, describe or draw flyer and poster layouts, write briefs for image tools and review designs before they go out.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 4 team members. Start by giving the lead (`design-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `design-lead` | Design Lead | Plans design work, splits it between the team, reports |
| `brand-designer` | Brand Designer | Moodboards and palette proposals with hex values |
| `layout-designer` | Layout Designer | Flyers and posters as described layouts or SVG files |
| `image-brief-writer` | Image Brief Writer | Prompts and briefs for image tools |
| `design-reviewer` | Design Reviewer | Reviews designs before they go out |

## Sample tasks

1. **Propose a colour palette for a new bakery brand** (for `brand-designer`): Suggest three palettes of five colours each, with hex values, names and where to use each colour, for a small bakery with a warm, friendly personality.
2. **Lay out a flyer for a design studio open day** (for `layout-designer`): Describe, or draw as an SVG file, an A5 flyer for an open day at a small design studio: headline, date, address placeholder, image area and a call to action.
3. **Compose image briefs for five blog illustrations** (for `image-brief-writer`): Compose a clear brief for each of five blog illustrations about coffee roasting, covering subject, style, colours, format and what to avoid, ready to paste into an image tool.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
