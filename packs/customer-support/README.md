# Customer support office pack

Draft replies in your brand tone, write FAQs, sort and tag exported tickets and summarise customer feedback. Replies are drafts; nothing is ever sent for you.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 4 team members. Start by giving the lead (`support-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `support-lead` | Support Lead | Plans support work, splits it between the team, reports |
| `reply-drafter` | Reply Drafter | Drafts answers in the brand tone; never sends |
| `faq-writer` | FAQ Writer | Writes FAQs and help texts |
| `ticket-triager` | Ticket Triager | Sorts and tags exported tickets |
| `feedback-summariser` | Feedback Summariser | Summarises customer feedback |

## Sample tasks

1. **Draft replies to three customer complaints about late delivery** (for `reply-drafter`): Using the three messages I paste in, draft calm, honest replies in our brand tone that apologise, explain next steps and avoid promises we cannot keep.
2. **Compose an FAQ page for a small online shop** (for `faq-writer`): Draft ten frequently asked questions and answers about shipping, returns and payment for an invented online shop selling hand-made candles, from the policy notes I provide.
3. **Sort last week's exported tickets by topic and urgency** (for `ticket-triager`): Take the ticket export in the folder I name, tag each ticket by topic and urgency, and list the three most common problems.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
