# HR office pack

Draft job ads, interview question sets and scorecards, first-week onboarding plans and handbook sections, all as drafts for a qualified person to check.

The pack adds one office to your Arsenale dashboard with a lead and 4 team members. Start by giving the lead (`people-lead`) a goal in plain words; it splits the goal into tasks for the others and reports back.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `people-lead` | People Lead | Plans HR work, splits it between the team, reports |
| `job-ad-writer` | Job Ad Writer | Writes clear, neutral job ads |
| `interview-planner` | Interview Planner | Question sets and scorecards |
| `onboarding-planner` | Onboarding Planner | First-week plans |
| `policy-drafter` | Policy Drafter | Handbook sections, marked as drafts |

## Sample tasks

1. **Draft a job ad for a part-time shop assistant** (for `job-ad-writer`): Compose a clear job ad for a part-time shop assistant at a small bakery: duties, hours, skills wanted, what we offer. Use neutral wording and mark items to confirm.
2. **Prepare an interview plan for a junior designer** (for `interview-planner`): Create a question set and a scorecard with clear criteria for interviewing a junior graphic designer at a small design studio.
3. **Plan the first week for a new office assistant** (for `onboarding-planner`): Draft a day-by-day first-week plan for a new office assistant: introductions, tools, training and a check-in at the end of the week.

## How to start

- Put the files the work needs (notes, exports, examples) in one folder and tell the lead where it is.
- Say what you want finished and by when; the lead turns it into tasks.
- Each member saves its result as a file in the folder you name and lists it in Arsenale as a deliverable.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts. They never send, post, pay or order anything for you; every such step stays with you, and Arsenale asks before any of them.
- A team member is a role your assistant takes on for one task, not a separate AI working in parallel. The exception is Claude Code, where members can run as separate sub-agents.
- Members only use files and sources you give them or allow. They do not ask for passwords, card numbers or bank details, and neither should you give them.
- Check every draft before you use it. Figures, names and dates come from what you provided; anything missing is marked TO CONFIRM.
- The output of this pack is not legal, tax or employment advice. It is a draft for a qualified person to check, and local law or local rules may apply.
